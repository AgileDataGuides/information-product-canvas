# SaaS Revenue Metrics Becomes the Default Example Canvas

**1 April 2026**

AgileDataGuides today released an update making the SaaS Revenue Metrics canvas the default example shipped with the Information Product Canvas. New users now open the app to a complete, real-world example covering MRR, ARR, ARPU, churn, expansion, and net revenue retention — instead of a blank canvas or a contrived starter example.

## The Problem

The original starter canvas was a thin demo that didn't show what a real, production-grade Information Product Canvas actually looks like. New users opened the app and either stared at a blank grid trying to guess what each section was for, or copied the demo's pattern verbatim and ended up with a tiny canvas missing most of the depth that makes the framework useful.

## The Solution

Ship a comprehensive SaaS Revenue Metrics canvas as the default. It models the kind of Information Product almost every SaaS company needs — the revenue dashboard that answers the CRO and CFO's questions about MRR, ARR, ARPU, churn, expansion, and net revenue retention. Every section is filled in:

- 1 persona (Chief Revenue Officer)
- 7 detailed business questions covering MRR trend, ARR by segment, ARPU, expansion, churn, NRR, channel contribution, and new vs. existing customer revenue mix
- 7 core business events feeding the dashboard (Prospect Clicks Ad → Trial → Activated → Customer Renews → Customer Churns)
- Multiple delivery types, vision statement, will/won't scope, features

The example reads end-to-end like a real briefing document — copy-edit it, replace the SaaS-specific bits with your domain, and it's a usable starting template.

## How It Works

The example JSON ships in `data/saas-revenue-metrics.json` and seeds the app on first launch when no other canvases exist. It also seeds the live demo's localStorage so visitors landing on the GitHub Pages version see the same fully-populated example.

## Key Benefits

- **Less blank-canvas paralysis** — new users see what "good" looks like immediately
- **Realistic depth** — every section shows what level of detail belongs there
- **Copy-and-edit starting point** — many teams' first canvas is a lightly-edited version of this one
- **Demonstrates the value** — the canvas proves itself by showing how much shape it gives a real revenue product

The SaaS Revenue Metrics canvas is available now in the Information Product Canvas app.
