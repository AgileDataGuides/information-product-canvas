# Information Product Canvas Adds Built-In Instructions and Claude Prompts

**31 March 2026**

AgileDataGuides today released an Instructions tab for the Information Product Canvas, giving users guided prompts for filling in their canvas and using it with Claude.

## The Problem

New users opening the Information Product Canvas for the first time face a blank canvas with twelve sections to fill in. Without guidance, it is not obvious where to start, what makes a good business question, or how to write effective vision statements. Teams also wanted to use Claude to review and improve their canvases but were not sure what questions to ask.

## The Solution

The new Instructions tab sits alongside the Canvas tab and provides three categories of guidance:

- **Quick Start** — questions based on the included SaaS Revenue Metrics example, helping users understand each section by seeing a completed canvas
- **Build Your Own** — prompts for reviewing and improving your own canvas, such as checking alignment between personas and business questions, identifying gaps, and suggesting scope decisions
- **Claude Chat** — ready-to-use prompts for Claude, including drafting concept models, suggesting metrics, identifying alternative delivery types, and breaking large canvases into smaller ones

## How It Works

Click the **Instructions** tab at the top of the app. Browse the prompt categories and click any prompt to use it as a starting point. Export your canvas as JSON and paste it into Claude alongside the prompt for AI-assisted review.

## Key Benefits

- **Guided onboarding** — new users know exactly where to start
- **Better canvases** — structured prompts help teams think through requirements more thoroughly
- **Claude integration** — pre-written prompts get value from AI without prompt engineering
- **Learn by example** — the SaaS Revenue Metrics example demonstrates what a complete canvas looks like

The Instructions tab is available now in the latest release.
