# The Information Product Canvas Now Guides a Team Through It One Step at a Time

**2 November 2026**

AgileDataGuides today released a 12 Steps tab for the Information Product Canvas app, so a team can fill in the canvas one area at a time, in the order the Green Book gives and with the book's advice beside each area, instead of facing twelve empty boxes at once.

## The Problem

The Information Product Canvas showed all twelve sections on one page: Name, Product Owner, Vision, Outcomes/Actions, Personas, Business Questions and the rest. That page is the right view once the canvas is full. For a team starting from a blank canvas, it gave no way in. People did not know which box to fill first, so they jumped around, skipped the Won't list, and argued about the Name and the T-Shirt Size before anyone had agreed what questions the product must answer. The Green Book, an Agile Data Guide to Information Product Canvas, sets out the twelve areas in an order, but a Facilitator running a session had to hold that order in their head. The Concept Model app already walked a team through its method one step at a time, and the Information Product Canvas had nothing like it.

## The Solution

The app now opens on a **12 Steps** tab, with the **Canvas** tab beside it. Twelve steps run down the left, one for each area of the canvas, each with a line that reads off the canvas, for example "3 Personas" or "No Business Questions yet". Click a step and its panel opens on the right with the book's page for that area: the question the book asks, its one line on what the area holds, its advice on how to draw the answers out of a stakeholder, an example from the book's own canvas, and a Read more link to the book's articles on the area. Under that is the add field. Type an answer, press Enter, and the cursor stays in the field for the next one. A Back and Next bar under the panel names the step before and the step after. The Canvas tab still shows the whole canvas on one page.

## How It Works

The steps follow the Green Book's "First things first, Last things last" sequence: Business Questions, Outcomes / Actions, Personas, Delivery Types, Data Sync, Core Business Events, Feature Stories, Will / Won't, Information Product Name, Product Owner, Vision and T-Shirt Size. The book says the areas can be completed in any order that works; this is the order Shane Gibson refined over a decade of Pattern Storming sessions. Business Questions come first because they are the easiest to draw out. The Vision comes second to last because it needs the other areas to write. The T-Shirt Size comes after the workshop, with the data team. The steps are a checklist, never a wizard. Every step is one click away at any time, and no step locks another. The Steps tab and the Canvas tab hold the same cards, so a card added in a step appears on the canvas at once, and a click on any card edits it the same way on both tabs. The Steps tab also shows inside the Context Plane, beside the canvas.

## Key Benefits

- **A way in for a blank canvas**: the team answers one question at a time, in the Green Book's order
- **The book beside the work**: every step carries the book's question, its advice, its example and a link to its articles on that area
- **One language on both tabs**: the section names on the canvas now match the book, "Outcomes / Actions", "Data Sync", "Will / Won't"
- **Built for the room**: Enter adds and keeps the cursor, so a Facilitator can type as fast as people talk
- **Progress at a glance**: every step shows how far it has got, so gaps like an empty Won't list are easy to see
- **Nothing to relearn**: the same cards, the same edit popup, and the full canvas one tab away

The 12 Steps tab is available now in the Information Product Canvas app in the Context Plane monorepo, and it reaches the public app at [github.com/AgileDataGuides/information-product-canvas](https://github.com/AgileDataGuides/information-product-canvas) on its next publish.
