# Information Product Canvas Now Exports to PowerPoint

**31 March 2026**

AgileDataGuides today announced PowerPoint export for the Information Product Canvas, enabling teams to share their canvas with stakeholders who prefer slide decks.

## The Problem

Not everyone on a data team uses the same tools. Business stakeholders, sponsors, and governance boards often expect requirements to be presented in slide format. Manually recreating an Information Product Canvas in PowerPoint is tedious and error-prone, and the slides quickly fall out of sync with the working canvas.

## The Solution

The Information Product Canvas now generates PowerPoint slides directly from the canvas with a single click. Each Information Product becomes its own slide, laid out to match the AgileData Google Slides IPC template.

The export runs entirely in the browser — no server-side processing, no uploads, no external services. The generated file downloads directly to the user's machine.

## How It Works

Click **Export PPTX** in the toolbar. The app generates a `.pptx` file with:

- A title slide with the model name and description
- One slide per Information Product, showing all canvas sections
- A green header bar with the product name, owner, and t-shirt size
- Vision statements displayed as pill badges (FOR / WHO / THE / THAT / UNLIKE)
- CC BY-SA 4.0 and AgileDataGuides branding in the footer

## Key Benefits

- **One click to slides** — no manual slide creation or copy-paste
- **Always in sync** — regenerate slides whenever the canvas changes
- **Runs locally** — no data leaves your machine
- **Professional layout** — matches the AgileData template for consistent presentation

PowerPoint export is available now in the latest release.
